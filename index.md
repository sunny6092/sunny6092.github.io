## 💡 Key features

### Simple photo viewing
The main window displays your images in a clean grid. You can adjust the size of thumbnails to see more or fewer items at once. Click any image to view it in full size.

### Duplicate detection
The app scans your selected folders for duplicate files. It compares the content of your photos to find exact matches. You can choose to delete duplicates to free up space on your hard drive.

### Smart sorting
Organize your library by date, file type, or folder location. The app groups your videos separately from your photos. This makes it easy to find specific memories without scrolling through years of data.

### Bulk renaming
Change the names of multiple files at once. You can add dates or custom text to groups of photos to keep your folders tidy.

### Culling tools
Use the keyboard shortcuts to flag or reject photos. This feature helps you identify blurry or poor-quality shots so you can remove them quickly.

## 📋 Tips for better organization

Keep your photos in folders named by year and month. For example, create a folder called "2023" and place folders like "01-January" inside it. When you open these folders in LinTabSort, the software keeps this structure intact. Always keep a backup of your original files on an external hard drive or a cloud storage service before you delete anything from your main computer.

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting common issues

If the application fails to open:

* Re-download the file from the release page to ensure it is not corrupted.

If the application runs slowly:

If you cannot see your images:
* Check that you selected the correct folder path in the settings menu.
* Ensure your files are in a standard format like JPG, PNG, or MP4.
